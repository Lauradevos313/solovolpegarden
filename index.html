This website doesn't use analytics or tracking cookies. However, some external websites and services connected to it may ask your browser to store data. A few things worth knowing:

  • Hosting: This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit, GitHub records your IP address for security purposes.
  • Digitaal Tuintje webring: This website is part of the Digitaal Tuintje webring, which loads a small script from its server. This means your browser connects to the Digitaal Tuintje server. According to the person who manages it, visitor data is not stored.
  • External links: Some buttons take you to other websites, which have their own privacy policies and data practices.

What happens if you decline? If you don't give consent, the play button that takes you to external websites will be disabled. The contact section will still be available, but any external links you choose to open are at your own discretion.
